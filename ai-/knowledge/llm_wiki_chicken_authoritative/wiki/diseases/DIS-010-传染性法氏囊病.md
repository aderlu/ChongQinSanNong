# 传染性法氏囊病

## 病原与分类

- Reference title: Infectious Bursal Disease in Poultry
- The etiologic agent of infectious bursal disease is the infectious bursal disease virus (IBDV), a double-stranded RNA virus (*Avibirnavirus gumboroense*) in the family Birnaviridae.
- As with other birnaviruses, IBDV virions are nonenveloped and have an icosahedral single-shelled geometry.

## 中国监管状态

- China official category III animal disease

## 典型宿主与阶段

- Two serotypes of IBDV have been identified.
- The **serotype 1 viruses cause disease in chickens** and, within them, antigenic variation can exist between strains.

## 临床症状

- Depending on the IBDV strain and presence of maternal immunity, infectious bursal disease can cause clinical or subclinical disease in young chicks.
- Results of infection depend on age and breed of chicken and virulence of the virus.
- Infections before 3 weeks old are usually subclinical.
- For both clinical and subclinical forms of the disease, all pathogenic IBDVs cause lesions in the cloacal bursa (bursa of Fabricius).

## 剖检变化

- For both clinical and subclinical forms of the disease, all pathogenic IBDVs cause lesions in the cloacal bursa (bursa of Fabricius).
- Gross pathology photograph showing an enlarged, hemorrhagic cloacal bursa in a chicken infected with very virulent infectious bursal disease virus.
- This strain of the virus can also cause hemorrhages in skeletal muscles.

## 实验室诊断

- Primary diagnosis-standard anchor: GB/T 19167-2020 传染性法氏囊病诊断技术
- Detection of macroscopic and microscopic lesions in the cloacal bursa
- Detection of IBDV viral RNA
- Initial diagnosis of infectious bursal disease is accomplished by the observation of gross lesions in the cloacal bursa.
- This is followed by microscopic analysis of the bursa for lymphocyte depletion in the follicles.

## 防控要点

- There is no treatment for infectious bursal disease.** Therefore, control and prevention are key.
- Rigorous disinfection of contaminated farms after depopulation has achieved limited success.
- IBDV is very stable in the environment and difficult to eradicate from premises.
- Live, attenuated virus vaccines of chicken embryo or cell-culture origin and of varying low pathogenicity can be administered by eye drop, drinking water, or SC routes at 1–21 days old.
