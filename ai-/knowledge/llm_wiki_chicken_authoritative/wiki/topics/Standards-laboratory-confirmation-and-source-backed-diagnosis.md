# Topic: Standards, laboratory confirmation, and source-backed diagnosis

## What this page is for

- Use this page when you need confirmation-grade evidence, national or industry standards, or laboratory reference paths.
- Diseases requiring CN standards: 60 | diseases already linked to standards: 16.

## Recommended workflow

- Open a disease page first to identify the target condition.
- Then move to a linked standards source page and verify the standard number, jurisdiction, and extraction quality.
- If the standard page is weak or missing, keep the case in a provisional state and record the gap in the issue queue.

## Evidence guardrails

- Only standards or authoritative source pages should be used for confirmation-style claims.
- Do not substitute a general topic or a bulletin summary for a standard anchor when a formal diagnosis statement is needed.
