# Topic: Differential diagnosis and disease navigation

## What this page is for

- Use this page when the disease is not yet fixed and you need a clear path from syndrome bucket to candidate disease pages.
- Current disease categories: 16.

## Recommended workflow

- Start from the broad category section below, then open disease pages with standards anchors first.
- Use diseases without a primary source only as temporary leads until the evidence layer is repaired.
- When two diseases look similar, favor the one with stronger CN standards or a clearer regulatory anchor.

## Evidence guardrails

- Category grouping is navigational, not diagnostic proof.
- A disease page with `standards > 0` has stronger confirmation support than a page with only a generic source.
