# Topic: Authority audit and source verification workflow

## What this page is for

- Use this page when you need to judge whether a claim is authoritative enough to keep in the wiki or to serve downstream generation.
- Authority levels: A0=57, A1=8, A2=54, B1=2.
- Evidence roles: clinical_reference=57, diagnosis_standard=35, epidemiology=1, product_label=6, regulation=22.

## Recommended workflow

- Trace every important claim back to a source page and then to the raw original.
- Prefer CN official, standards, and primary evidence over derivative summaries.
- If a page has no primary source, no standards anchor, or appears in the manual queue, mark the claim as provisional.

## Evidence guardrails

- Topic pages are never the final provenance layer.
- When evidence and summary disagree, trust the raw source and repair the compiled page.
