# Topic: Management, nutrition, toxin, and non-drug interventions

## What this page is for

- Use this page when the first-line answer may be environment correction, management action, nutrition adjustment, or toxin control rather than medication.
- Management-oriented disease pages: 10.

## Recommended workflow

- Open management-like disease pages before recommending drugs for stress, environment, nutrition, or toxin patterns.
- Confirm whether the page has a primary local source; if not, avoid strong prescriptive language.
- Escalate to drug pages only when the management scenario clearly includes a compliant pharmaceutical step.

## Evidence guardrails

- Non-drug scenarios should not be forced into a medication answer.
- Use toxin and nutrition pages to rule out false infectious framing before escalating to reportable disease logic.
