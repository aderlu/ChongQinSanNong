# Topic: Medication legality, withdrawal, and egg-stage compliance

## What this page is for

- Use this page when a case includes any treatment proposal, withdrawal question, egg-stage restriction, or label verification need.
- Drug summary pages: 44 | rule-backed summaries with the highest compliance load are listed below.

## Recommended workflow

- Start from the ingredient summary page, not the disease page, when the question is whether a drug can be used.
- For each ingredient, verify restriction type, production stage, withdrawal days, and evidence URL.
- If verification status is not official or still requires label verification, treat the answer as incomplete and surface the uncertainty.

## Evidence guardrails

- Do not infer legality from clinical habit or common use.
- When `needs_label_verification` appears, keep the recommendation provisional until the label or bulletin source is confirmed.
