# Topic: Regulatory reporting, quarantine, and immunization obligations

## What this page is for

- Use this page when the case may trigger mandatory reporting, classification, quarantine, or immunization obligations.
- Official-status disease pages: 7.

## Recommended workflow

- Check the disease page for official China status.
- Open the linked rule anchor before making any claim about reporting, quarantine, culling, or mandatory immunization.
- Keep product-label and drug rules separate from disease-reporting obligations.

## Evidence guardrails

- Only CN official rule anchors should support reporting or legal-duty statements.
- If a disease is suspected but not confirmed, keep the reporting language conditional until confirmation evidence is established.
