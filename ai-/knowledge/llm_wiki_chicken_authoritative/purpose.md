# Purpose

- Build a compliance-aware, source-grounded chicken disease wiki for dataset generation and review.
- Prioritize persistent synthesis over one-off retrieval: disease pages, drug pages, and source pages should accumulate stable knowledge.
- Treat raw evidence as immutable and the wiki as the maintained interpretation layer.
- Prefer fewer, denser navigation pages over many shallow duplicates.


构建具备合规意识、溯源可查的鸡病专业知识库，用于数据集生成与审核。优先采用持续知识整合模式，而非一次性检索：病害词条、药品词条及来源词条需沉淀稳定知识内容。原始证据保持不可篡改，知识库作为可维护的解读解析层。精简导航页面、提升内容密度，避免大量浅层重复页面。
