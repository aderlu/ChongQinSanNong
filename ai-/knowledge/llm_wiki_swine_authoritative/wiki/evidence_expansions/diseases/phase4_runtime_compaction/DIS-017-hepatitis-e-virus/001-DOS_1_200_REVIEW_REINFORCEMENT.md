## Diseases of Swine 1-200 local-md review reinforcement / V15

> Added 2026-05-08. This block uses local Markdown evidence from raw/md/DISEASES OF SWINE1-200.md and source pages SRC-0003 to SRC-0014 as a review-safe retrieval aid. It intentionally does not change evidence_status: NEEDS_REVIEW; it narrows diagnosis, sampling, differential, treatment, and regulatory boundaries for generation and evaluation.

### Local evidence anchors

- SRC-0003 Herd Evaluation: herd history, records, four-circle site visit, diagnostic question formation, intervention priority, outcome monitoring, blood and oral-fluid collection.
- SRC-0007 Differential Diagnosis of Diseases: system-based differentials for digestive, respiratory, skin, hemopoietic, neurologic, locomotor, reproductive, zoonotic, vesicular, and urinary presentations.
- SRC-0008 Diagnostic Tests: culture/AST, ELISA, antigen detection, IHC, MAT, PCR/qPCR/multiplex PCR, sequencing, toxicology, virus isolation, neutralization, and test-performance interpretation.
- SRC-0009 Sample Selection: representative animals, antemortem/postmortem samples, necropsy, thoracic, abdominal, reproductive, CNS, and locomotor tissue collection, plus necropsy safety.
- SRC-0010 Evidence and Causality: pathogen or toxin detection is not causality unless compatible with clinical pattern, lesions, timing, epidemiology, sample quality, and differential exclusion.
- SRC-0011 Disease Control: host-pathogen-environment ecology, transmission routes, within-farm and between-farm biosecurity, transport, live/dead pigs, feed, people, footwear, clothing, equipment, and untreated food boundaries.
- SRC-0012 Drug Therapy: treatment objective, route/logistics, regulatory constraints, withdrawal/residue avoidance, prudent use, resistance limitation, and treatment-failure reassessment.
- SRC-0014 Food Safety and Zoonotic Interface: use for public-health, residue, feed-safety, and zoonotic framing; jurisdiction-specific legal actions still require jurisdictional official sources.

### Crosscutting use rule

For this page, local Markdown under raw/md/ may substitute for pathogen-chapter, diagnosis, regulatory-boundary, and disease-chapter evidence when it is cited with source/page/chapter scope. It must not be expanded into executable drug dose, fixed withdrawal period, MRL, slaughter/transport order, or local legal conclusion unless a label-level or jurisdiction-specific source is also linked.

### Viral disease boundary

- Diagnostic answers should combine production stage, herd spread, morbidity/mortality, clinical syndrome, lesions, and laboratory evidence; a PCR-positive, serology-positive, or virus-detection result alone is not enough to prove causality.
- Sampling should follow the syndrome: respiratory cases need acute respiratory tract/lung-oriented samples; enteric cases need feces, intestinal contents, and intestinal tissue; reproductive cases need fetuses, placenta, and sow/gilt samples; neurologic cases need CNS collection under veterinary safety guidance; vesicular or zoonotic suspicions need escalation and official-source review.
- Differential diagnosis should route through the relevant syndrome/comparison page before final labeling, especially for diarrhea, respiratory disease, reproductive failure, vesicular lesions, neurologic signs, sudden death, and zoonotic exposure.
- Control advice should prioritize isolation, movement-risk control, sanitation, dead-pig handling, vehicle/equipment/personnel biosecurity, and investigation of introduction routes. Supportive care may be mentioned only within veterinary and regulatory boundaries; antimicrobial treatment must not be framed as antiviral cure.
