## Supported Conclusions

- APHIS states that vesicular stomatitis mainly affects horses and cattle, and can occasionally infect swine, sheep, goats, llamas, alpacas, and people.
- APHIS states that vesicular stomatitis is primarily transmitted by biting flies and midges.
- APHIS lists blister-like lesions around the mouth, nose, sheath, udders, ears, and coronary band, with lameness possible when the coronary band is affected.
- APHIS states that animal health professionals report diagnosed or suspected cases of nationally listed reportable animal diseases to APHIS Area Veterinarians in Charge and to the State animal health official as applicable under State reporting regulations.
- APHIS states that the disease can impact animal movement and international trade.
