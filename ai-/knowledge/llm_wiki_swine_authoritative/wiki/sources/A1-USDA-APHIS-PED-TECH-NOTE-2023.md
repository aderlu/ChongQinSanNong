# Porcine Epidemic Diarrhea (PED) Technical Note

## Supported Conclusions

- USDA APHIS describes PED's pathogenic agent as a coronavirus distinguishable from transmissible gastroenteritis of swine only by laboratory tests.
- USDA APHIS states that PED is most serious in neonatal piglets, where morbidity and mortality can be 80 to 100 percent.
- USDA APHIS states that PED transmission is fecal-oral and that no vector or reservoir has been implicated in its spread in the technical note.
- USDA APHIS states that economic loss occurs through death and production loss, plus vaccination and biosecurity costs.
- USDA APHIS states there is no effective treatment other than control of secondary infections.
