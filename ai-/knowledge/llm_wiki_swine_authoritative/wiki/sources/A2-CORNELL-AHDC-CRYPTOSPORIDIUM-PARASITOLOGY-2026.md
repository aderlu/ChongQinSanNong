# Cornell AHDC: Parasitology Testing Protocols and Interpretations

## 来源

- URL: https://www.vet.cornell.edu/animal-health-diagnostic-center/testing/testing-protocols-interpretations/parasitology
- 发布机构：Cornell University College of Veterinary Medicine, Animal Health Diagnostic Center
- 访问日期：2026-05-12
- 权威等级：A2

## 可支持结论

- Cornell AHDC describes Cryptosporidium ELISA as a fecal procedure detecting Cryptosporidium-specific antigen.
- Cornell AHDC runs Cryptosporidium ELISA in parallel with double centrifugation concentration flotation for Cryptosporidium oocysts and other fecal parasites.
- Discordant ELISA and flotation results require interpretation as possible low-level shedding, false positive, or false negative, and Cornell recommends collecting a second sample to resolve discordant results.
- Cornell AHDC advises submitting sufficient sample and including animal health history with the sample.
