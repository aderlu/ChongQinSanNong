# Iowa State VDL: Interpretation of Parasitology Results - Swine

## 来源

- URL: https://vetmed.iastate.edu/vdl/laboratories/parasitology/swine/
- 发布机构：Iowa State University Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratory
- 作者：Matthew T. Brewer, DVM, PhD, Dipl. ACVM
- 访问日期：2026-05-12
- 权威等级：A2

## 可支持结论

- Swine fecal flotation provides a broad overview of nematode and protozoan parasites and reports semi-quantitative results.
- Iowa State VDL states that parasitology reports in pigs require critical interpretation before herd-level recommendations.
- Other differential diagnoses for the clinical sign of concern must be ruled out; rare or few parasites in fecal flotation are generally not clinically significant.
- A fecal exam from a single animal or few animals from a large population is unlikely to represent the status of the whole group; at least 10 same-age-class samples are recommended for herd evaluation.

## 不得外推边界

- This source supports interpretation boundaries for swine parasitology diagnostics. It does not establish that Cryptosporidium is the cause of diarrhea in a specific case without compatible clinical, herd, lesion, and ancillary diagnostic context.
