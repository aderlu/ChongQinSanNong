# GB 13078-2017 Hygienical standard for feeds as posted by MOA

## Source

- URL: https://www.moa.gov.cn/ztzl/sqxzjcgs/xzjcbz/202506/t20250630_6475267.htm
- Accessed: 2026-05-07
- Publisher: Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs
- Jurisdiction: China

## Usable boundary

- Use for China feed hygiene limits and listed test-method references for feed contaminants, including mycotoxins, mould count, bacterial count, and Salmonella in feed/feed ingredients.
- Use for feed-safety boundary statements in pig disease datasets where contaminated feed is a risk or differential factor.

## Do-not-extrapolate boundary

- Do not infer clinical signs, toxic dose, diagnosis, or treatment of mycotoxicosis from the standard.
- Do not apply feed-product limits to pet food/additives or to products outside the standard's stated scope.
