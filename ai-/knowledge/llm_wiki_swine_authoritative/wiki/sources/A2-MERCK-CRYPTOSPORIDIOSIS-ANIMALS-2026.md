## 可支持结论

- Cryptosporidiosis is a GI parasitic disease caused by Cryptosporidium species; oocysts contaminate water and food and are difficult to eliminate from the environment.
- In pigs, C parvum, C suis, and C scrofarum are reported from 1 week old through market age; most infections are subclinical, but infection can contribute to malabsorptive diarrhea in nursing and postweaning piglets.
- Infection is transmitted by fully sporulated infective oocysts excreted in feces; direct animal-to-animal, fomite/human-mediated, environmental, feed, and water contamination routes are described.
- Diagnosis can use acid-fast stained fecal smears, fecal flotation, ELISA, lateral flow assay, direct immunofluorescence assay, or PCR.
- Treatment is supportive; strict hygiene and reduction of environmental contamination are central to control.
