## Usable Boundary

- Use for rabies laboratory diagnosis boundaries: animal rabies confirmation is laboratory-based and relies on appropriate postmortem neural tissue testing by qualified laboratories.
- Use to block answers that diagnose rabies from clinical signs alone or that advise unsafe handling of suspected rabid animals.
