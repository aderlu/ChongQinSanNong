# Iowa State Veterinary Pathology: Parasitology Services

## 来源

- URL: https://vetmed.iastate.edu/vpath/services/diagnostic-services/parasitology-services/
- 发布机构：Iowa State University Department of Veterinary Pathology
- 访问日期：2026-05-12
- 权威等级：A2

## 可支持结论

- Fecal flotation with Sheather's solution and centrifugation is listed for detection of ova, cysts, and oocysts from fresh feces.
- When Giardia or Cryptosporidium is suspected, Iowa State recommends submitting both unfixed feces and a similar quantity of formalin-fixed feces.
- Fecal samples must not be frozen.
