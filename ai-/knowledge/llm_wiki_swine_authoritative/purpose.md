# Purpose

This Wiki stores auditable swine disease, drug, rule, source, comparison, syndrome, and synthesis knowledge for case generation, answer evaluation, arbitration, retrieval routing, and golden dataset production.

The runtime principle is source-first and task-aware: a fact can be used when its source is clear, the data is valid, the authority level matches the requested use, and the mapped task-use status allows that workflow. Legacy review-oriented fields such as `HUMAN_REVIEWED` and `NEEDS_REVIEW` are migration signals, not standalone usability gates.

High-risk regulatory, drug-label, dose, route, course, withdrawal-period, MRL, residue, food-safety, reporting, quarantine, culling, or movement-control claims require A0 or label-level equivalent support. Unsupported high-risk claims must be downgraded to boundary language, routed for source expansion, or blocked.
