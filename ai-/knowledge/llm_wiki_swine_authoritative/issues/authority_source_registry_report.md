## Source policy

- A0 sources are China official MOA or MOA Animal Husbandry and Veterinary Bureau pages. Use these first for China-specific prevention/control, statutory, feed-safety, and surveillance boundaries.
- A1 sources are WOAH disease pages, Code chapters, or Terrestrial Manual chapters. Use these for international disease identity, reportability/listed disease framing, diagnostic-manual boundaries, and food-safety/zoonotic interfaces.
- A2 sources are CDC, EFSA, FDA, or GOV.UK. Use them only for public-health, food-safety, occupational exposure, or risk-2aee4f263dad2d98046bb67515e900c0 boundaries. They are not primary sources for swine herd treatment or China animal-health policy.
- Non-authority pages were not used to generate facts.

## Known limitations and guardrails

- PDCoV: EFSA is an official risk-2aee4f263dad2d98046bb67515e900c0 source, but not a WOAH disease page and not China policy. Do not generate China farm control facts for PDCoV from this source alone.
- Streptococcus suis: GOV.UK is useful for zoonotic/public-health exposure, but should not be used for swine clinical treatment. Prefer textbook or China veterinary official material for pig-disease facts if later located.
- MOA seasonal guides are useful for China-context prevention points, but they are not disease-specific technical standards and should not be used to invent detailed protocols.
- Feed-safety standards can support contaminant-limit and governance statements, but not pig clinical toxicology unless a disease/toxicology source is added.
